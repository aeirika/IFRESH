Analysis Results

The iFRESH device uses sensors and a camera to determine if the meat is fresh, stale, or spoiled.

Fresh Phase

Low VOCs, standard oxymyoglobin color.

Stale Phase

Rising NH3, metmyoglobin shift detected.

Spoiled Phase

Critical H2S levels, structural degradation.

User Guide

Select your role to see the steps for using iFRESH.

For Consumers

  1. Open Meat Inspection and click Open Camera.
  2. Select Pork or Beef, then capture the meat sample when the camera feed is connected.
  3. Review sensor readings and inspection results.
  4. Browse markets and stalls in Market Vendors.
  5. Open Market Map to view iFRESH test locations.
