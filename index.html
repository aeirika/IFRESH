Analysis Results

The iFRESH device uses sensors and a camera to determine if the meat is fresh, stale, or spoiled.

Fresh Phase

Low VOCs, standard oxymyoglobin color.

Stale Phase

Rising NH3, metmyoglobin shift detected.

Spoiled Phase

Critical H2S levels, structural degradation.
